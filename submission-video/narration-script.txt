Covenant is a non-custodial performance attestation layer for autonomous agents. Every result the agent produces gets hashed into Merkle roots, committed on-chain, and left open to challenge from anyone. It's built in Rust on Arbitrum Stylus, and USDG is both the bond and the unit of account.

And this is not a prototype. The contract is live on Arbitrum Sepolia, deployed and source-verified. Address zero x nine two six one three e eight four. A real Stylus activation, thirty-three Rust tests behind it, and it answers reads right now.
